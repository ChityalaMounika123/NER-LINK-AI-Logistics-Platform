# NER-LINK AI

## AI-Based Smart Logistics and Accessibility Intelligence Platform for the North Eastern Region of India

You are the lead full-stack software engineer, ML engineer, GIS engineer, DevOps engineer, and technical architect for this project.

Your task is to build a complete, production-style prototype called **NER-LINK AI**.

Do NOT create a fake/demo-only application with hardcoded random values.

Build a modular, scalable, well-documented system with a working frontend, backend, database, GIS/map functionality, GPS tracking, weather integration, field incident reporting, ML-based risk prediction, route recommendation, alerts, role-based authentication, offline synchronization, real-time updates, testing, and deployment configuration.

The application must be understandable and suitable for a Smart India Hackathon prototype and product-company technical interviews.

---

# 1. PROBLEM STATEMENT

The North Eastern Region (NER) of India has difficult terrain, heavy rainfall, landslides, floods, road damage, bridge failures, traffic disruptions, limited connectivity, and other accessibility challenges.

These conditions can make transportation of essential goods difficult and unpredictable.

The proposed system is an AI-powered logistics and accessibility intelligence platform designed specifically for the North Eastern Region.

The platform should:

1. Monitor road and transportation accessibility.
2. Track essential-goods vehicles using GPS.
3. Collect weather and environmental information.
4. Allow field officers to report road incidents with GPS coordinates and photographs.
5. Detect and represent blocked or risky roads.
6. Predict the probability of road/logistics disruption using machine learning.
7. Analyze possible routes.
8. Recommend safer/suitable alternative routes.
9. Consider both travel time and disruption risk.
10. Consider cargo priority when recommending routes.
11. Alert authorities and drivers about important disruptions.
12. Provide a centralized GIS dashboard for government/administrative users.
13. Support multilingual notifications.
14. Support offline-first field reporting and later synchronization.
15. Integrate external data sources through proper APIs.
16. Clearly distinguish LIVE, PUBLIC, GOVERNMENT, FIELD, and SIMULATED data.

IMPORTANT:

The system is NOT intended to replace Google Maps, government systems, GPS providers, or existing disaster-management systems.

Instead, NER-LINK AI acts as an intelligence and decision-support layer that integrates available data sources and provides predictive, logistics-focused analysis for the NER.

The major innovation is:

Existing systems may provide maps, navigation, weather information, GPS tracking, or disaster information separately.

NER-LINK AI combines these sources and adds:

```
Risk Prediction
    +
Route Risk Analysis
    +
Cargo Priority
    +
Accessibility Intelligence
    +
Field-generated Incident Intelligence
    +
Decision Engine
```

to recommend safer and more suitable logistics routes.

# 30. PRIORITY-AWARE ROUTING

Example:

Route A:

```
ETA = 180 minutes
Risk = 82%
```

Route B:

```
ETA = 240 minutes
Risk = 25%
```

For:

```
NORMAL cargo
```

the system may prefer Route A.

For:

```
CRITICAL medical supplies
```

the system should strongly consider Route B.

The recommendation must explain WHY.

Example:

```
Recommended Route B because it has significantly lower
disruption risk for critical cargo, despite a longer ETA.
```

Do not simply say "AI selected this route."
